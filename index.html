Transforming Careers Through Practical Education

Founded in 2015, Eastman Technical Training Institute has grown into a premier technical college located conveniently along the Embu-Meru Highway. We are dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality, and market-driven vocational training.

Our Mission

To equip students with practical skills, innovative mindset, and professional attitude required to excel in the global job market.

Our Vision

To be the leading technical and vocational training institute in Kenya, renowned for innovation, competence, and integrity.

Course / License Class Duration Certification Key Training Modules

Class D Full Course Full Course NTSA Driving License Bus and PSV driving, passenger safety, road awareness, confidence on the road
Motorcycle As per package NTSA Motorcycle License Balance control, handling, road safety, traffic awareness
